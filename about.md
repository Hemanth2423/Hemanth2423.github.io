## My path

I started in aerospace engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, where I also founded a food-tech venture that reached 1,000 users in six months with funding from the IIT Madras incubator. Since then, I've built products across corporate benefits, conversational AI, and life sciences. I'm now pursuing an MBA at UC Berkeley Haas while working in AI product management.

I enjoy the mix of careful problem framing, close collaboration with technical teams, and bringing useful products into the hands of real people.
